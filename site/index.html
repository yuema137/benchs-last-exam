About and acknowledgement

BLE thanks benchmark-radar by Koutian Wu, which tracks where AI benchmark and evaluation results are reported. We use it only as a reference for discovering sources: every number in BLE is re-read from the original paper, leaderboard or vendor release and cited there, and no benchmark-radar content is copied or redistributed.

The two projects answer different questions. benchmark-radar helps you find benchmarks and the places their results appear, across a very large collection. BLE is deliberately selective: it follows a small set of classic, widely followed benchmarks and evaluates the benchmarks themselves, along dimensions such as discriminative power, headroom and saturation speed, coverage and freshness, protocol comparability, cost and contamination resistance, so you can judge what a benchmark still tells you about frontier models.
